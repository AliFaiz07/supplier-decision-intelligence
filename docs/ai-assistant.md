# Controlled Procurement AI Assistant | SAP Supplier Decision Intelligence

## 1. Architectural Philosophy & Zero-Hallucination Guardrails

The **Procurement AI Assistant** (`backend/app/core/assistant_service.py` & `frontend/src/components/assistant/AssistantWidget.tsx`) is designed specifically for analytical procurement inquiries.

### Critical Operating Principle
The AI Assistant does **NOT** calculate scores, invent supplier metrics, or determine rankings. 

- **Deterministic Layer Handles**:
  - Multi-Criteria Decision Analysis (MCDA) mathematical scoring
  - Supplier eligibility verification (SAP vendor master, plant relevance, capacity constraints)
  - Effective landed procurement cost modeling
  - Statistical price volatility, delivery OTIF, and defect PPM aggregations
  - Anomaly detection and sensitivity thresholds
- **AI Assistant Handles**:
  - Natural language domain query parsing
  - Retrieval and synthesis of structured application metrics
  - Grounded comparative explanations
  - Evidence citation and audit trail linking

Under no circumstances does the assistant invent PO numbers, contract values, unit prices, or quality records.

## 2. Supported Domain Query Archetypes

The assistant natively resolves the core analytical query categories specified in Master Prompt Section 32:

1. **Material Sourcing Recommendations**:
   - Query: *"Which supplier should I consider for M-001?"*
   - Engine Action: Retrieves requirement parameters, runs deterministic MCDA, and synthesizes landed cost, OTD, defect rate, and contract coverage with evidence citations.

2. **Supplier Positive Justification**:
   - Query: *"Why is SUP-004 preferred?"*
   - Engine Action: Retrieves empirical metrics for the supplier and explains why its multi-dimensional score outperforms peer alternatives.

3. **Supplier Penalty & Exclusion Analysis**:
   - Query: *"Why is SUP-002 not preferred?"*
   - Engine Action: Retrieves concrete penalty factors (e.g., elevated rejection rate, substandard OTD, active risk events, or vendor block).

4. **Delivery Performance Inquiries**:
   - Query: *"Which supplier has the best delivery performance?"*
   - Engine Action: Aggregates verified Goods Receipts (MSEG) and ranks suppliers by verified on-time delivery (OTD) percentage.

5. **Contract Coverage Queries**:
   - Query: *"Which suppliers have an active contract for M-003?"*
   - Engine Action: Queries `contracts.json` and details outline agreement IDs, agreed prices, rebates, and validity periods.

6. **Performance Deterioration Inquiries**:
   - Query: *"Show me suppliers whose performance deteriorated recently."*
   - Engine Action: Queries trailing 90-day delivery and trailing 60-day quality deterioration telemetry to identify declining vendors.

7. **Priority Weight Trade-Off Simulation**:
   - Query: *"What happens if delivery becomes more important than price?"*
   - Engine Action: Runs the decision engine under `Balanced` vs `Delivery-Focused` priority weights and explains the ranking shift and cost premium trade-off.

8. **Head-to-Head Supplier Comparison**:
   - Query: *"Compare SUP-002 and SUP-007."*
   - Engine Action: Generates side-by-side analytical comparisons across quoted price, effective landed cost, OTD rate, defect PPM, and risk ratings.

9. **Single-Source Vulnerability Audits**:
   - Query: *"Which materials have single-source risk?"*
   - Engine Action: Inspects the SAP source list and flags materials with $\le 1$ approved vendor, detailing facility spend and exposure level.

10. **Price Anomaly Detection**:
    - Query: *"Where has procurement price increased significantly?"*
    - Engine Action: Identifies purchase orders where the latest price deviates $\ge 10.0\%$ above historical baseline distribution bands.

11. **Contract Lifecycle Expiry Tracking**:
    - Query: *"Which contracts are expiring soon?"*
    - Engine Action: Details purchasing agreements expiring in $<30$ days requiring urgent commercial renewal.

12. **High-Spend Vendor Deterioration**:
    - Query: *"Which suppliers have high spend but declining performance?"*
    - Engine Action: Cross-references high annual spend ($>€30\text{k}$) with active quality or delivery degradation signals.

## 3. Evidence Packaging & Auditability

Every response generated by the assistant includes an explicit **Evidence Sources** audit list referencing canonical datasets:
- `purchase_orders.json` (SAP EKKO/EKPO)
- `goods_receipts.json` (SAP MSEG/MKPF)
- `quality_records.json` (SAP QALS/QMEL)
- `contracts.json` (SAP Outline Agreements)
- `risk_events.json` (Operational Disruption Log)

Additionally, the assistant provides contextual **Suggested Actions** (e.g., *"Open Sourcing Workspace"*, *"Inspect Evidence Panel"*, *"Filter by Contract-Focused Priority"*) enabling direct navigation across intelligence cockpits.
