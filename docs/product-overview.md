# Product Overview: SAP Supplier Decision Intelligence

## 1. Executive Summary

**Supplier Decision Intelligence** is an enterprise-grade procurement decision support layer designed around core SAP S/4HANA procurement and manufacturing paradigms.

It bridges transactional ERP execution and strategic sourcing intelligence by aggregating multi-dimensional signals—commercial pricing, Goods Receipt (GR) telemetry, Quality Management (QM) inspection results, outline agreements, financial solvency, and concentration exposures—into an explainable, deterministic multi-criteria decision framework.

The system does **not** replace the buyer or the ERP; rather, it empowers procurement professionals with an auditable, simulation-driven workspace answering:
> *"Given this procurement requirement, supplier history, pricing, delivery performance, quality, contracts, risk, and business priorities, what sourcing option should the procurement team consider, why, and how robust is that choice under adverse conditions?"*

```text
COMMERCIAL & RISK ANALYTICS

        ↓

```

## 3. Five Core Enterprise Pillars

1. **Overview & Cockpit**: Real-time procurement KPIs, urgent requirements queue, and cross-portfolio health indicators.
2. **Sourcing Workspace**: Dynamic requirement configuration, progressive disclosure of technical constraints, deterministic multi-criteria decision evaluation, single vs. multi-supplier quota allocation, sensitivity thresholds, inline stress testing, and explainable AI audit rationale.
3. **Supplier 360 Deep Profile**: Complete 24-month spend, verified OTIF reliability, defect PPM, outline agreements, "What Changed?" MoM/QoQ drift telemetry, and risk signal feeds.
4. **Analytics Hub**: 
   - **Spend Analytics**: 24-month expenditure trends, category distributions, and contract compliance.
   - **Price Intelligence**: Historical price bands, volatility metrics, and contract vs. spot variance.
   - **Delivery Intelligence**: Rolling OTIF, delay distributions, and lead time variance.
   - **Quality Intelligence**: Inspection lot results, defect categorizations, and vendor PPM ratings.
   - **Outline Contracts**: Validity tracking, commitment utilization, and uncontracted spend leaks.
   - **Risk Intelligence**: Financial stability, concentration warnings, and single-source dependencies.
   - **Procurement Anomaly Center**: Real-time detection of price jumps, delivery slippage, quality spikes, and contract expiries.
   - **Predictive Forecasting**: 6-month material price trends (with 95% confidence bands), supplier reliability forecasting, and quarterly spend projections.
5. **Decisions Hub**:
   - **Scenario Simulator**: What-If MCDA priority adjustments across Price, Delivery, Quality, Lead Time, Contract, and Risk.
   - **Decision Stress Testing**: Systematic testing against 8 adverse supply chain shocks and supplier outage simulations.
   - **Procurement Memory**: Closed-loop evaluation of expected vs. actual outcomes with automated lessons learned.
   - **Decision Audit History**: Tamper-evident ledger of every award decision, configuration snapshot, and buyer override.

## 4. Key Architectural Differentiators

* **Strict Separation of Concerns**: Deterministic mathematical scoring (MCDA) calculates recommendations; the AI assistant interprets and explains grounded empirical facts without hallucinating metrics.
* **Effective Total Landed Cost**: Moves beyond raw quote prices by factoring in prompt-payment cash discounts, outline rebates, transport logistics, and empirical non-performance defect costs.
* **Deterministic Stress Testing**: Evaluates decision durability against severe macroeconomic shocks before committing enterprise funds.
* **Closed-Loop Memory**: Tracks post-award reality against original sourcing assumptions, enabling continuous organizational learning.
